Global SaaS logo standards

Four benchmarks you named — what each mark does, and the pattern they all share. Your current OnTime lockup is at the bottom for side-by-side calibration.

Gradient action mark

ClickUp

  • Mark = an upward arrow + semicircle: the product promise ("click up") drawn as one symbol
  • Vibrant multi-stop gradient identity (#FF02F0, #FC6D2D, #6647F0, #0091FF)
  • Geometric wordmark, capitalized "U" enforced by brand rules

Minimal monochrome mark

Linear

  • Diagonal rounded streaks forming a sphere — pure geometry, no literal meaning
  • Single indigo (#5E6AD2) — remarkably close to Pragmr's own #514EB3
  • Quiet custom wordmark; the whole identity is restraint

High-contrast data mark

ClickHouse

  • Geometric logomark built from data-column bars — the database drawn as structure
  • Two-color system: neon yellow #FAFF69 on near-black #0A0A0A
  • Punchy contrast pairing; logomark reserved for small/symbol contexts

Friendly geometric shapes

monday.com

  • Three simple shapes in a row — circle, dome, diamond — days progressing toward done
  • Warm tri-color (red/yellow/green) + lowercase wordmark for approachability
  • Shapes are so simple a child could redraw them — that is the test

Your product — already on standard

OnTime by Pragmr™

  • Endorsed lockup: authentic Pragmr™ mark | simple divider | ⊙nTime with the ring-tick O
  • Two colors (indigo + ink), flat vector, geometric — same class as the four above
  • The ring-tick O is your "click-up equivalent": the promise drawn as a symbol

What makes these "global standard"

  • One simple mark that survives 16px favicon and billboard scale — flat geometry only, no 3D, no thin lines
  • One color, or one gradient — never a rainbow of unrelated hues
  • A clean geometric wordmark locked to the mark with fixed clear space
  • The mark encodes the product promise (up, progress, structure, days) — decoration is banned
  • Same assets everywhere: favicon, app icon, social, deck — one source of truth
